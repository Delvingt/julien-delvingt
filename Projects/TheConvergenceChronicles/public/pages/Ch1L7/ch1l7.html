Chapter 1: The Drain

Self-Regulating Processes

The new water tank from last mission works — but there's a problem. "It has no drain!" Without an outlet, the tank fills forever and eventually overflows. The engineers install a drain valve at the bottom.

This creates a self-regulating process. As the level rises, water pressure pushes more flow out through the drain. Eventually, inflow equals outflow and the level reaches a steady state. The tank is now modeled as a first-order system: 1/(s+1).

Notice how the response curves toward a final value instead of ramping in a straight line. This exponential approach is the signature of a first-order system.

Objective

Drag the Valve from the Toolbox onto the canvas. Wire Pump → Valve → Leaky Tank → Level Sensor. Set the valve gain so the tank settles to a steady-state level of at least 1.0. Click "Run Simulation" to observe the response.
