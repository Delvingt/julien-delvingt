Chapter 1: Not Enough Water!

Combining Flow from Multiple Sources

The Convergence Institute's irrigation system is in trouble. The main water channel requires a steady flow of 2.0 L/s to keep the crops alive — but each pump alone can only deliver 1.0 L/s.

"We've installed a second pump," says the Head Engineer, "but we need someone to connect them both into a single pipe. The flows must be combined before reaching the sensor."

It's up to you, Engineer. Drag the Pipe Connector from the Toolbox and wire both pumps through it to reach the required flow.

Objective

Drag the Pipe Connector (Add block) from the Toolbox onto the canvas. Wire both Pumps into the connector, then wire it to the Flow Sensor. Achieve exactly 2.0 L/s of combined water flow.

Multiple Inputs

1 / 1

Some blocks can accept more than one input. Notice the Pipe Connector has two input ports on its left side — one for each pump.

Wire each pump to a separate input port, and the block will add their flows together.
