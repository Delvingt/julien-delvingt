Chapter 1: Our Control Valves Broke Down!

Finding the Right Combination

Disaster! The valves you used last time have broken down. Both pumps are still running at 1.0 L/s each, and the channel still needs exactly 1.5 L/s — but you have no way to adjust the flow smoothly.

Luckily, a neighbouring farm has lent you three pipe obstructions — fixed restrictors that reduce flow by set amounts: ×0.80, ×0.80, and ×0.94. Each can only be used once.

Figure out the right combination of obstructions to place on the pipes so the combined flow reaches 1.5 L/s!

Objective

Use the Pipe Connector and the available Obstructions from the Toolbox. Wire both pumps through to the Flow Sensor so the combined flow reaches 1.5 L/s (within ±0.1).
