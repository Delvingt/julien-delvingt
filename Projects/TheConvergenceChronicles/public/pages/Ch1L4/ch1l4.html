Chapter 1: Too Much Water!

Regulating Combined Flow

The irrigation system from last time is back online — but now there's a new problem. The crops downstream are being flooded! Both pumps are running at full power, delivering 2.0 L/s combined, but the channel can only handle 1.5 L/s.

"We need to throttle at least one of the pumps," the Head Engineer explains. "Use a Valve to reduce the flow before it combines in the pipe."

There's more than one way to do this, Engineer. Experiment with different valve positions to find a configuration that works!

Objective

Use the Pipe Connector (Add) and at least one Valve (Gain) from the Toolbox. Wire both pumps through to the Flow Sensor, adjusting the valve(s) so that the combined flow reaches exactly 1.5 L/s.
