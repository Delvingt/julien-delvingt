Chapter 1: The Water Source

Understanding Input-Output Relationships

Welcome to The Convergence Chronicles

You're about to learn the fundamentals of process control engineering through interactive simulations. Every control system starts with a simple relationship: an input that affects an output.

Welcome, Engineer, to the Convergence Institute.

Before you can control complex systems, you must understand the most fundamental concept: how inputs affect outputs.

In front of you is a simple water system. A pump controls the flow of water through a pipe. Speed up the pump, and water flows. Stop it, and the flow stops. The relationship is direct and instantaneous.

Your task is simple: set the pump to achieve exactly 1.0 L/s of water flow.

Objective

The Pump and Flow Sensor blocks are connected in the Flow Editor. Adjust the pump slider until the flow sensor reads exactly 1.0 L/s

Your Control Engineering Workspace

1 / 3

Your workspace has two main areas:

  • Flow Editor (Left): Where you'll see the pump and flow sensor blocks
  • Results Panel (Right): Shows your current flow reading and submit button

For this level, everything is already set up - you just need to adjust the pump!

Understanding the Blocks

2 / 3

In the Flow Editor, you'll see two connected blocks:

Pump Speed: Source block with output port (right side)
Flow Sensor: Sink block with input port (left side)

The wire connects pump output to flow sensor input.

Achieving Your Target

3 / 3

To complete this level:

  1. Find the Pump block with the slider
  2. Drag the slider to adjust the pump speed
  3. When the flow reaches 1.0 L/s, click check flow in the bottom simulation
  4. Submit your model!

Hint: What pump speed gives you a flow of 1.0?
